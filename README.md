## 📱 Anime & TV Series Tracker

**Project Overview**  
A cross-platform mobile application (Android & iOS) designed to help fans track their anime and TV series watchlists, monitor progress, and engage with communities. Built with performance, simplicity, and fan engagement in mind.

**✨ Objectives**
- Intuitive and mobile-friendly UI/UX  
- Seamless episode progress tracking  
- Community engagement via clubs, forums, and polls  
- AI-powered recommendations based on viewing history  
- Lightweight, responsive, and optimized mobile experience  

**🚀 Core Features**
- **Menu Categories:** All, Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to Watch  
- **Progress Tracking:** Log watched episodes, track remaining ones  
- **Community Clubs & Discussions:** Create clubs, invite members, spoiler-protected forums  
- **Polls & Ratings:** Vote on episodes, characters, and storylines  
- **User Reviews & Comments:** Rate and review shows  
- **Reminders & Notifications:** Upcoming episodes, scheduled viewing alerts  
- **Streaming Integration:** Direct links to streaming platforms  
- **Offline Mode:** Save watchlists and notes without internet  
- **Sharing:** Share watchlists with friends and communities  
- **Dark & Light Mode:** Theme customization  
- **Tags & Filters:** Genre, language, release year, etc.  
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Graphs of viewing history and total watch time  

**🛠 Deliverables**
- Fully functional mobile app (Android & iOS)  
- Backend database for user data  
- Responsive UI/UX  
- Admin panel for content & user management  
- Documentation & user guide
